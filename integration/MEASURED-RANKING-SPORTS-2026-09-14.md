# Measured quality ranking and smaller sports navigation

User authorized switching from provider-label ranking to measured quality plus editorial popularity preferences after completion of the 1,701-feed audit.

`venom-measured-ranking.py` runs at the end of every approved-channel build, after availability exclusions. It reads channel-quality-audit.json and writes per-feed evidence to channel-measured-ratings.json. Publication uses the existing native-ID manifest and Live TV Categories plugin, shared with the Kodi category browser; no application rebuild or skin patch is required. No device-side UI verification was performed during deployment.

Ranking: successfully measured feeds first, resolution tier, colour precision/HDR evidence, progressive high-frame-rate motion, editorial recognition, exact height and deterministic name/ID ties. This is not a subjective visual-quality assessment or measured audience popularity. Bitrate is retained in the original audit but not compared across different codecs as a simplistic quality score. Provider 8K/4K labels do not influence the final rank. Unknown colour metadata gains no HDR boost. At least 10-bit plus BT.2020 plus PQ/HLG is required for the strongest colour tier; 8-bit HLG is recorded as signalled but unverified, without an HDR bonus. Measurements older than seven days are unverified for ranking until retested.

The general ar-sport ID is preserved as a 48-feed Sports picks section, with one preferred feed per normalized family where possible. Full remaining sports feeds remain available in beIN, Sky, TNT/BT, Arabic networks, US/Canada, events and other English/Arabic sections, split into pages of at most 100 feeds. Existing dedicated sports categories remain. Explicit named-team feeds supplement baseball/basketball; other additions require sport-specific channel names. No claims are made about current live schedules on general-purpose sports channels. Obvious movie/kids/drama/series feeds accidentally swept into sports are moved to Provider entertainment, retaining IDs. Explicit Italian/French markers surviving older display-name cleanup are excluded from the custom lists.

Official network context: https://www.skysports.com/more-sports/news/32414/10947050/what-to-watch-on-new-sky-sports-channels distinguishes dedicated channels from mixed-sport Action/Arena. Consequently we do not blindly copy every mixed sports channel into every dedicated-sport list.

Tests verify measured resolution beats inflated labels, unknown/8-bit-HLG gets no HDR bonus, full feed coverage across smaller sections, bounded network sections and repeatable application. Deployment dry run showed no lost feeds before the explicit language filter. Initial network split had 730 sports feeds; counts can change as the background checker promotes or suppresses channels.

The API caches category snapshots for approximately five minutes; old counts can remain visible briefly after publication. Personal favourites, provider channel identities, artwork and playback URLs are not changed. Old provider-label ordering modules remain staging helpers only; final publication ordering is now the measured-ranking pass.

Publication exposed a renumbered Jellyfin channel: name+old number no longer resolved Dubai Sama. Resolver now permits a previously verified gateway-to-native ID when the current cleaned name still matches case-insensitively; it does not guess from name alone. Missing/ambiguous mappings are omitted and logged in unresolved-native-channels.json rather than blocking the entire catalogue. Initial publication omitted one unresolved new feed (USA 5 StarMAX). It is retried on subsequent publication. Final checks: 37 published groups, 48 Sports picks, all published group orders match the measured source manifest. Local curation tests: 15 passing; measured-ranking tests: 3 passing.
